When a patient pays, that money doesn't all belong to the clinic. Some of it may go to the lab, some to the treating dentist. The system works this out at the moment the payment is taken.

## The order things come out

**Lab fee first, then the dentist's percentage, and the clinic keeps the rest.**

The dentist's commission is calculated on what's left *after* the lab is paid — not on the full amount the patient handed over.

Worked through with a 5,000 crown, an 1,800 lab fee, and a dentist on 30%:

| | |
|---|---|
| Patient pays | 5,000 |
| Less lab fee | −1,800 |
| **Net** | **3,200** |
| Dentist's 30% of the net | −960 |
| **Clinic keeps** | **2,240** |

If the commission were taken on the full 5,000 instead, the dentist would get 1,500 and the clinic 1,700 — the clinic absorbing the entire lab bill on its own side. Taking it off the top first is what splits the lab cost fairly between them.

## The rule that surprises people

**The lab fee comes off the first payment only.**

When a patient pays in instalments, the lab fee is deducted once — from the earliest payment against that treatment. Every later instalment is split between dentist and clinic with no further lab deduction.

Same crown, paid in two halves of 2,500:

| | First payment | Second payment |
|---|---|---|
| Paid | 2,500 | 2,500 |
| Lab fee | −1,800 | 0 |
| Net | 700 | 2,500 |
| Dentist 30% | 210 | 750 |
| Clinic | 490 | 1,750 |

The dentist earns **less on the first instalment and more on the second**. That's correct — the lab is only paid once — but it looks wrong to a dentist checking their own numbers mid-treatment. Worth explaining before they ask.

## When the payment is smaller than the lab fee

If a patient pays 1,000 against a crown with an 1,800 lab fee, the net is negative. **The dentist earns no commission on that payment** — there's nothing left after the lab. They earn on the following instalments instead.

Nothing is lost; it's deferred.

## Where the numbers come from

- **Lab fee** — set on the service in your price list (*Requires lab* and *Estimated lab fee*). See the pricing article.
- **Commission percentage** — set per dentist.

Both are read at the moment the payment is recorded. Changing a service's lab fee later does **not** retrospectively change payments already taken.
