Everything the patient-level screens record adds up here.

## Where to find it

**Finance** in the sidebar.

![The Finance page](/help/en/finance-ledger.png)

## True Net — the headline

The big number is **True Net**: what the clinic actually keeps, *after doctor and lab deductions and recorded expenses*. Not what came through the till.

It's built from four lines, shown underneath it:

| Line | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Cash in** | Payments actually received |
| **Discounts** | Given away on procedures |
| **Commissions + lab** | The dentists' share and the lab's, taken out of cash in |
| **Expenses** | Anything you've recorded as going out |

The same four appear as cards across the top, so you can see each on its own.

**Look at True Net, not Cash in.** A day with 20,000 through the till and 14,000 of lab bills and commission is a very different day from 20,000 with none — and only True Net tells them apart.

## Cash in counts money, not work

A 5,000 crown fitted today but not paid for adds nothing. A patient settling a bill from three months ago adds 5,000 today.

So a busy month can look quiet, and a quiet month can look excellent, purely from when patients happened to pay. If you want to know how much *work* you did, that's the reports section — this screen answers "what came through the till, and what did we keep".

## Choosing the period

**Daily · Monthly · Range**, with a date picker beside them. Daily opens on today.

## The transaction list

Every movement in the period, with **Date, Details, Patient, Allocations and Amount** — income in green, expenses in red.

Narrow it with:
- **All / Income / Expense**
- **Doctor** — one dentist's takings only
- **Search** by patient or note

## Recording an expense

Press **Manual Entry**. This is where rent, salaries, materials and lab bills go in — anything the clinic pays out that isn't already tracked.

Be consistent with how you describe them. Always "Lab", never sometimes "Laboratory" and sometimes "lab fees" — that consistency is the difference between a breakdown you can read and a list you can't.

## Exporting

**PDF Report** produces a formatted report — the totals, an income-versus-expenses comparison, both transaction tables and a doctor commission breakdown. Suitable for handing to an accountant.

> Exporting is its own permission. Someone can be allowed to view Finance without being able to download it — see *Roles and permissions*.
