The dashboard is the screen you land on. It's built to answer one question in three seconds: **what is happening in the clinic right now?**

![The dashboard](/help/en/dashboard.png)

## The numbers along the top

| | What it counts |
|---|---|
| **Daily Income** | Money **received** today |
| **Appointments** | How many are booked today |
| **Appointments Status** | Today's split — Confirmed, Unconfirmed, Completed |

**Daily Income is cash, not work done.** A patient who had a 5,000 crown fitted today but hasn't paid adds nothing to it. A patient settling an old bill adds their payment. See *The clinic's ledger* for why that distinction matters.

Under the status split there's a plain-language read of how the day is going — *"Flow is smooth & steady"* and similar.

## Today's schedule

The middle of the screen is the day, hour by hour, with each appointment showing the patient, their phone, the treatment, the dentist and the duration.

This is also where you run the day without opening anything: each appointment carries a one-click button for its next step — **Arrive**, **Seat**, **Check Out** — and the mood tag sits beside the patient's name. See *Running the day*.

**Day / Week** switches the range, and **View All** opens the full calendar.

## Attendance

If you haven't clocked in, the dashboard says so in pink, with a **Clock In** button. It's a nudge rather than a block — nothing stops you working without clocking in, but the hours won't be recorded.

## Quick actions

**New Patient** and **Quick Pay** sit at the top right. Between them they cover most of what a receptionist does in a day without navigating anywhere.

## Print Rx

Above the schedule. Prints a prescription for a patient you pick — the shortcut for when someone comes back to collect one.

## What it doesn't tell you

The dashboard is deliberately about **today**. Trends, comparisons and per-dentist performance live in Reports; totals across a period live in Finance.

If a number here looks wrong, check it in the ledger before worrying — the dashboard shows today's cash, which is a narrower thing than most people assume at first glance.
