Booking is the busiest thing the front desk does. One switch inside it decides whether money lands on the patient's account, so it's worth knowing which one.

## Where to find it

Open **Appointments** and press **Add Appointment**. The booking form opens as a panel down the left, with the calendar still visible beside it — so you can see what's already booked while you choose a time.

![The booking panel beside the week calendar](/help/en/booking-form.png)

## Step 1 — Find the patient

Type a **name or a phone number** into the Patient box. Matching patients appear underneath with their number; click the right one and it locks in.

If nobody comes up, the patient has no file yet. Tick **New Patient** at the top of the panel to create one without leaving the booking.

## Step 2 — Add the treatment

Press **Add Procedure**. This is where the visit gets its content.

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Primary reason for visit** | Pain, Check-up, Cleaning, Follow-up, Broken tooth, Cosmetic consultation, Emergency — the list you set in Settings |
| **Service** | Pick from your price list. The cost fills in automatically |
| **Cost** | Fills from the service; type over it if this case is priced differently |
| **Add to Ledger** | **The switch that decides whether the patient gets charged** |

Then press **Confirm Procedure** to attach it to the appointment.

### About "Add to Ledger"

It's ticked by default, which is right most of the time — the patient is having work done, so the work gets billed.

**Untick it when the visit shouldn't cost anything.** A review after a root canal, a fitting appointment in a course of treatment already paid for, a patient coming back because something needs adjusting. The appointment still gets recorded, the dentist still sees it, but nothing is added to what the patient owes.

This is the one to get right in both directions. Leave it ticked on a free follow-up and the patient is charged twice for one job. Untick it on real new work and the treatment never gets billed at all — and nobody notices until you're chasing a balance that was never raised.

## Step 3 — When, who, how long

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Date** | |
| **Time** | The dropdown only offers times inside your clinic hours |
| **Dentist** | Only staff marked as dentists appear. If it's empty, nobody is set up as one yet |
| **Duration** | Defaults to 30 minutes |
| **Appointment stage** | Starts at **Unconfirmed**. Set it to Confirmed straight away if the patient is on the phone agreeing to it |

## Step 4 — Confirm booking

Press **Confirm Booking**.

Two things can interrupt, and both ask rather than block:

**"Clinic closed this day"** — that date is a day off in your schedule. Fine for a deliberate exception; if it appears unexpectedly, check your working days.

**"Slot already taken"** — that dentist is already booked then. Sometimes intentional, usually a mis-click.

## Adding more than one treatment

Press **Add Procedure** again to attach another. Each one gets its own service, cost, and its own **Add to Ledger** choice — so a visit can quite correctly contain one billable procedure and one free follow-up.
