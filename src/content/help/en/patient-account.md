When a patient asks "how much do I still owe?", this is the screen that answers it.

## Where to find it

Open the patient's file and go to the **finance** section.

![A patient's account with its three totals and transaction history](/help/en/patient-finance.png)

## The three numbers

| | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Total Treatment** | Everything ever charged to this patient |
| **Total Paid** | Everything they've ever paid |
| **Balance Due** | The difference — what they owe right now |

If Balance Due is zero, they're square. If it's negative they've paid ahead and have credit sitting on the account.

## Transaction history

Below the totals, every line on the account in date order — date, description, type, cost, paid.

Charges and payments sit in the same list, which is what makes it readable: you can follow a crown being charged at 5,000, then a 2,000 payment, then 3,000 later, and see the balance close.

## Adding a payment from here

Press **Add Payment** (the form is headed **Receive Payment**).

- **Link to Procedure** — attach the payment to a specific treatment, or leave it as **General Payment**.
- **Description / Notes** — optional, but worth using for anything unusual: *"paid by her brother"*, *"deposit for whitening"*.
- **Amount (EGP)**.

Press **Confirm**.

This does the same job as Quick Pay on the dashboard. Use whichever you're nearer to — Quick Pay when the patient is at the desk, this when you're already looking at their file.

## Giving the patient a receipt

There are two documents, and they are not the same thing.

- **The receipt for one payment.** Every payment line has a printer icon. It opens the print dialog on a numbered receipt for that payment alone: the receipt number, what the money was for, how it was paid, what is still owed on that treatment and the account balance after it. Receipt numbers run in order and never repeat, so this is the paper the patient keeps. If the clinic has switched on *Open the print dialog as soon as a payment is saved* under Settings → Receipt, it comes out by itself the moment a payment is recorded.
- **Print statement**, above the history, prints the whole account: every treatment, every payment, the balance. Hand it over when someone asks "where do I stand?".
- **Send Receipt on WhatsApp** sends a text version of the statement straight to the patient's number.

How the paper looks — colours, font, paper size, language, which lines print, the tax details — is set once under **Settings → Receipt** and applies to both.

WhatsApp sending needs WhatsApp set up for your clinic, and it goes to the number on the patient's file — which is the practical reason the phone number has to be right when you create them.

## Fixing a mistake

Each line has an edit action. You can correct a wrong amount, a wrong description, or a payment attached to the wrong treatment.

Correct errors rather than deleting them where you can. A corrected line still shows the money moved; a deleted one leaves a hole that nobody can explain three months later when the totals don't match.

> Editing and deleting money records are separate permissions. A receptionist who can take payments can't necessarily undo them — which is usually how you want it. See *Roles and permissions*.

## Chasing an unpaid balance

There's no separate debtors screen. To find out who owes you, sort or filter the patient list, or use the reports section — then open each patient's account and work from the balance shown here.

The phone number is on the patient's file, so calling them is one screen away.
