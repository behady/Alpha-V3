> Attendance isn't on the free trial. If **Attendance** isn't in your menu, that's why.

## Where to find it

**Attendance** in the sidebar. The page is called **My Worksheet**.

**Every member of staff can open it** — it's one of only two screens that need no permission at all, because everyone has to be able to clock themselves in.

![The attendance worksheet](/help/en/attendance.png)

## Clocking in

Press **Clock In**.

The screen says: *"Ensure you are inside the clinic to securely clock in."* That's not just advice — clocking in is **location-checked**. Your clinic's position and an allowed radius are set in Settings, and the system checks you're inside it.

So it won't work from home, from the car park if your radius is tight, or if location is switched off on the device. If clocking in fails and you're standing in the clinic, that's usually the radius being too small — see the attendance settings.

Press it again at the end of your shift to clock out.

## My Tracker

**My Time Logs** lists your own history — date, clock in, clock out, total time, status. New staff see *No records found* until their first shift.

Above it, **Today · This Week · This Month · Custom Range** changes the period.

Check your own hours before payday, not after. A missed clock-out is far easier to sort out the same week than at the end of the month.

## Team Overview

The **Team Overview** tab shows everyone's attendance rather than just yours.

This one **is** gated: it needs the *Attendance admin* permission. Without it a member of staff sees only their own record — which is the right default. See *Roles and permissions*.

## What it's for

The page describes itself as *"Track shifts, log attendance, and run payroll invoices"* — the hours recorded here are what payroll is worked out from.

Which means a missed clock-in isn't a small thing: it's an hour of someone's pay with no record behind it.
