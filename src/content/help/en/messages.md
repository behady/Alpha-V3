WhatsApp does not let any outside program send messages for a clinic. So the system does the part it can: it decides **who** to message and writes **what** to say. A person still presses send.

That is what this screen is — a short list of messages already written, waiting for someone to send them.

![Messages waiting to be sent](/help/en/messages.png)

## Sending one

Click the green **Send** button on a message. WhatsApp opens — on a computer that's WhatsApp Web, on a phone it's the WhatsApp app — with the whole message already typed in the right chat.

You press send in WhatsApp. Nothing to type, nothing to copy.

The message then disappears from this list by itself, so nobody sends it twice.

## What lands here

- **New lead replies** — someone asked through a Facebook ad and the clinic chose to answer by hand rather than automatically (see *Leads*).
- **Appointment reminders** — the nightly job prepares tomorrow's reminders while nobody is at a screen.
- Anything else the clinic switched on in Settings → WhatsApp.

## The other buttons

**Dismiss** removes a message without sending it — for the ones you decide against, or already handled by phone.

**Sent** (top right) shows what has gone out recently, in case you need to check.

## Why messages disappear on their own

A message older than three days is dropped from the list. A reminder about "your appointment tomorrow" is worse than useless when tomorrow has already passed — better to say nothing than to confuse a patient.

## If the list is always empty

That is normal for a clinic that sends automatically instead. Two reasons a message would appear here:

1. Settings → WhatsApp → delivery is set to **manual**, or
2. delivery is automatic but the WhatsApp connection failed — the message drops into this list rather than being lost.

If you expected automatic sending and things keep landing here, check the WhatsApp connection in Settings.
