> The Supply Store only appears if a supplier has been connected to your account. If **Supply Store** isn't in your menu, nothing is wrong — it hasn't been switched on for you.

## Where to find it

**Supply Store** in the sidebar, just under Inventory.

## How it works

1. **Browse or search.** Type part of a name or a product code. Use the dropdown to narrow it to one category.
2. **Add to the basket.** Press **Add to basket**, then use **−** and **+** for how many you want.
3. **Open the basket** with the button at the top right.
4. **Fill in the delivery details** — clinic name, a phone the driver can call, and the address. After your first order these fill themselves in.
5. **Send the order to the supplier.**

That's it. The order goes straight into the supplier's own system. His team will call to confirm, deliver, and **collect the money in cash at the door**.

## You do not pay through Alpha

There's no card field on this screen and there never will be while the arrangement is cash on delivery. Alpha doesn't take the money and doesn't hold it. You pay the driver.

## Following an order

The **Our orders** tab shows everything you've ordered and where it has got to:

| What it says | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Waiting for the supplier | Sent. He hasn't confirmed it yet. |
| Being prepared | He's packing it. |
| On hold | He's paused it — usually a stock problem. Call him. |
| Delivered | It arrived and was paid for. |
| Cancelled / Refunded | It isn't coming. |

The status comes from the supplier's own system, so it updates when he updates it, not when we do.

## Who can order

Two separate permissions, on purpose:

- **Supply store** lets someone open the shop and see prices.
- **Place supply orders** lets someone actually send an order.

Give the first to whoever needs to check what something costs, and the second only to whoever is allowed to spend. Set both under **Settings → Users**.

## Common questions

**The price changed between yesterday and today.** Prices come live from the supplier's shop. What you see is what he's charging right now.

**An item I wanted isn't listed.** Only items he has in stock are shown. Out-of-stock items are hidden rather than greyed out, so you don't order something that can't be sent.

**The total on the order is more than the basket said.** He may add delivery when he confirms. The basket shows the goods; his confirmation shows the final figure.

**It says "the store is not connected right now".** That's on our side, not yours. Contact support.
