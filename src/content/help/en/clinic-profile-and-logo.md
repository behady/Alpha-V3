Your clinic's own details live in one place, separate from your personal profile. Fill this in once, early — a few features later on read from it.

## Where to find it

**Settings → Clinic Management → Clinic Profile**

This link only appears if you are an **Admin**.

![Settings sidebar, with Clinic Profile under Clinic Management](/help/en/settings-clinic-profile-link.png)

> **Careful — "Profile" is not the same thing.** The first tab in Settings is called **Profile**, but that is *your own* account: your name, your phone, your photo. The clinic's details are the separate **Clinic Profile** page described here.

## What to fill in

| Field | What it's for | Required |
|---|---|---|
| **Clinic name** | The name of the clinic itself | Yes |
| **Clinic phone** | Your reception number | No |
| **Address** | Where the clinic is | No |
| **Google Maps URL** | A link that opens directions to you | No |
| **Google review link** | A link that opens Google's "write a review" form | No |
| **Logo** | Your clinic's logo image | No |

Only the clinic name is required. You can come back and add the rest at any time.

![The Clinic Profile form with all its fields](/help/en/clinic-profile-form.png)

## The two Google links are different

This trips people up, so it is worth being clear.

- **Google Maps URL** is for *directions*. It's the link a patient taps to find their way to you. Open your clinic on Google Maps, press Share, and copy that link.
- **Google review link** is for *asking for a star rating*. It opens the review form directly. It usually looks like `https://g.page/r/…` and comes from your Google Business Profile.

The review link is the one used when you send a patient a WhatsApp message asking them to rate you — that message inserts it wherever you put `{{google_link}}`.

If you only have one of the two, put it in the matching box and leave the other empty. Pasting a Maps link into the review box means patients get directions instead of a review form.

## Adding your logo

1. Press **Choose file** in the Logo box.
2. Pick a **PNG or JPG** image from your computer or phone.
3. A small preview appears straight away so you can check it's the right image.
4. Press **Save**.

The file is uploaded when you save, not when you pick it. If you pick an image and leave the page without saving, nothing is stored.

> **What the logo does today:** it is saved safely against your clinic, and it shows in the preview box on this page — but it does **not** yet appear on printed receipts, prescriptions, or anywhere else in the app. Upload it now so it's ready, but don't expect to see it on your printouts yet.

## Saving

Press **Save** at the bottom. You'll see *Saved* confirm it.

If you changed the logo, saving takes a few seconds longer while the image uploads. The button is disabled during that time — that's normal, just wait.

**If saving fails:**

- *Upload failed* — the image did not upload. Try a smaller file, or a different PNG/JPG.
- *Images only* — the file you picked is not an image. PDFs and documents are not accepted.
- *Sign in required* — your session ended. Sign in again and re-enter your changes.
