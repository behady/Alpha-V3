This is the very first thing that happens. It takes about two minutes.

## Before you start

You need an email address and a password of at least 6 characters. Nothing else — no card, no code.

## Step 1 — Create your account

1. Open the app. You land on the sign-in screen.
2. Press **Create account**.
3. Type your name, your email, and a password.
4. Press **Create account** again to confirm.

![The sign-in screen with the Create account option](/help/en/login-create-account.png)

## Step 2 — Tell the system who you are

Once your account exists, you get a screen with two choices. Pick the one that describes you.

### "I own the clinic — start a free trial"

Choose this if you are the dentist or the clinic owner. You become the **Admin** — the person who can see and change everything.

1. Type your clinic's name, for example *Nour Dental Clinic*.
2. Press **Create clinic**.

That's it. The system builds your clinic and takes you straight to the dashboard.

### "I work at a clinic — join an existing one"

Choose this if someone else already set up the clinic and you are joining their team.

1. Ask your clinic's admin for the **Clinic ID**. They find it under **Settings → Users → Clinic ID**.
2. Paste it into the box.
3. Press **Send join request**.

Your admin sees your request under **Settings → Join Requests** and approves it. You will not be able to get in until they do — sign in again after they tell you it's approved.

![The two choices: create a clinic, or join one with a Clinic ID](/help/en/onboarding-two-choices.png)

> **Do not create a second clinic if your clinic already exists.** Two clinics do not talk to each other — patients, appointments and money added to one will not appear in the other. If your colleagues are already using the system, always join with the Clinic ID.

## If it says "This is taking longer than usual"

You may see a message saying activation is slow, with a **Refresh page** button.

**Your clinic was created and is saved.** The system is only waiting for your access to switch on.

- Press **Refresh page**.
- Do **not** press *Create clinic* again. Pressing it a second time makes a second, empty clinic — and you would still be looking at the same waiting screen.

If refreshing does not get you in after a minute or two, sign out and sign back in.

## What you get on the free trial

| | Free trial |
|---|---|
| Team members | Up to 3, including you |
| AI assistant (the chat bubble) | Yes — 200 credits a month |
| WhatsApp messaging | Not included |
| Inventory | Not included |
| Staff attendance | Not included |

You can add the rest of your team and upgrade later. Nothing you enter now is lost when you change plan.
