This is the most important thing in Settings. Booking reads from it, charges come from it, and dentist commissions and lab fees are calculated off it.

Get it right once and everything downstream is right. Leave it empty and the front desk types prices by hand every single time.

## Where to find it

**Settings → Prices**

![The price list, one card per treatment](/help/en/prices-list.png)

Each treatment shows its price, and anything needing lab work carries a **LAB** tag with the lab fee — so you can see at a glance which treatments cost you money before they earn you any.

## Adding a treatment

Press **Add Treatment**.

![The New Treatment form with lab work ticked](/help/en/add-treatment.png)

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Treatment name** | What the front desk will pick from the list |
| **Price (EGP)** | The standard price. It can still be discounted per booking |
| **Typical duration** | Optional. Fills the appointment length automatically when this treatment is booked |
| **Procedure requires external Lab Work** | Tick if a lab is involved |
| **Estimated lab fee (EGP)** | Only appears once you tick lab work |

Press **Save to List**.

## The lab fee is not decoration

Ticking lab work and entering a fee changes how **every payment for that treatment is split**. The lab's share comes out first, and the dentist's percentage is worked out on what's left.

Leave the lab fee blank on a crown and the clinic silently absorbs the whole lab bill while still paying the dentist commission on the full amount. See *Dentist commission and lab fees* for the arithmetic.

Enter it as what the lab actually charges you, not what you'd like it to be.

## Typical duration is worth filling in

It's marked optional, and it's the field most worth doing anyway. With it set, booking a Root Canal automatically blocks 90 minutes instead of the default 30 — so the calendar tells the truth about how full the day is.

Leave it blank and every treatment books as 30 minutes, which is how a day that looks half-empty turns out to be overbooked.

## Changing a price later

Edit the treatment. **Changing a price does not change anything already charged** — past treatments keep the price they were booked at.

That's correct, and it's worth knowing: raising your crown price today doesn't retrospectively raise what last month's patients owe.
