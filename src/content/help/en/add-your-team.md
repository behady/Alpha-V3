Everyone who uses the system needs their own login. Never share one account between people: the system records who did what, and shared logins make that record useless.

There are two ways in. Both end at the same place.

## Where to find it

**Settings → Users** — Admins only.

![The Users screen showing team member cards](/help/en/settings-users-overview.png)

---

## Way 1 — You create the account for them

Best when you are sitting with the person, or setting up before anyone starts.

1. Go to **Settings → Users**.
2. Press **Add Team Member**.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name**
   - **Email** — this is what they sign in with
   - **Password** — at least 6 characters; tell them to change it later
   - **Role** — Admin, Dentist, Assistant or Receptionist
4. If the person is an Admin who also treats patients, switch on **Also works as dentist**.
5. Save.

They can sign in immediately with that email and password.

![The Add Team Member form](/help/en/add-team-member-form.png)

---

## Way 2 — They sign up and ask to join

Best when the person is not with you, or is already using their own phone.

**You do this:**

1. Go to **Settings → Users**.
2. Find the **Clinic ID** box and press **Copy**.
3. Send that ID to your colleague — WhatsApp is fine, it is not a password.

![The Clinic ID box with its Copy button](/help/en/clinic-id-box.png)

**They do this:**

1. Create their own account on the sign-in screen.
2. On the next screen, choose **I work at a clinic — join an existing one**.
3. Paste the Clinic ID and press **Send join request**.

**Then you do this:**

1. Go to **Settings → Join Requests**.
2. You'll see a card with their name, email and the date they asked.
3. Press **Approve**, or **Reject** to turn them down.

![A pending join request card with Approve and Reject](/help/en/join-request-card.png)

> **Approving is only half the job.** Everyone approved this way is added as an **Assistant with no permissions**, whatever job they actually do. They will sign in and find almost nothing. Go straight to **Settings → Users → Manage Access** and set their real role and permissions — see *Roles and permissions* for how.

## Things you'll run into

### "Staff Limit Reached"

Your plan sets how many people you can have. The free trial allows 3, including you. When you hit the limit, **Approve** stops working and a red warning appears. Upgrade your plan, or remove someone who has left.

### Changing someone's password

If a staff member is locked out, you can set a new password directly — no email needed.

1. On their card, press the **key** icon.
2. Type a new password of at least 6 characters.
3. Press **Force Update System**.

Tell them the new password and ask them to change it. This action is recorded in the activity log.

### Removing someone

Press the **bin** icon on their card and confirm. This removes their login and their staff record from the clinic.

You cannot delete yourself — the bin icon does not appear on your own card. That is deliberate: it stops a clinic from losing its last Admin.

### A card marked "Broken Profile" in red

This means the person's login exists but their staff record is missing, so the system cannot tell what they're allowed to do. It usually happens when an account was half-created.

The fix is to **delete the card and add the person again** using Way 1 above. Their email can be reused.

### They're in, but the menu looks empty

That is expected, and it is the next article. A new team member starts with almost nothing switched on, and you decide what they see.

## What's next

Set their role and turn on the parts of the system they need.
