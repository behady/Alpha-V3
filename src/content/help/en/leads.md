Someone calls asking how much a crown costs, says "I'll think about it", and hangs up. Before this page, that person vanished. Now they get written down — and followed up.

![The Leads inbox](/help/en/leads.png)

## What counts as a lead

Anyone who asked but hasn't booked: a phone call about prices, a WhatsApp message from an ad, a friend of a patient who "might come". If they're already standing at the desk booking, they're a patient — add them normally. Everyone else goes here first.

## Adding one

Tap **Add lead**. Name and phone are the only must-haves — the rest you fill if you know it:

![The New lead form](/help/en/add-lead.png)

- **Source** — where they came from: Meta ads, WhatsApp, Google, a friend. This one matters most. It's what the Marketing Funnel report counts, so pick it honestly every time.
- **Asked about…** — start typing and your own services list suggests itself.
- **Follow up on** — the day they should be chased. Leads due today or overdue float to the top of the list with an amber tag, so the morning routine is just: open Leads, work from the top.
- **Branch** — only shows if your clinic has branches.

## Working the list

Each lead is a card. The three round buttons open **WhatsApp**, dial the **phone**, or **convert to patient**. Tapping WhatsApp on a New lead marks it Contacted by itself.

The dropdown on the card moves the lead through its stages: **New → Contacted → Booked → In the chair**, or **Lost**. Marking a lead Lost asks for the reason — "too expensive" said thirty times is a pricing decision waiting to happen.

The tabs above the list filter by stage. **Active** (the default) hides won and lost leads so the list stays a to-do, not an archive.

## Converting

When a lead agrees to come in, tap the convert button. The app first checks the phone number against your patients — if they already exist, the lead links to them instead of creating a twin file. If not, a patient file is created carrying the lead's source, so the channel keeps its credit forever.

## The payoff: the Marketing Funnel report

**Reports → Marketing Funnel** turns all this discipline into an answer:

![The Marketing Funnel report](/help/en/marketing-funnel.png)

One row per channel: leads in, still in progress, lost, made it to the chair, the conversion rate — and the money those converted patients actually paid. A channel only gets revenue credit for money traceable to a recorded lead, so ads can't claim credit for walk-ins.

That last column is the number to put next to what each channel costs you. A channel bringing ten leads and no chairs is noise; one bringing three leads and two chairs is where the budget should go.

## Habits that make it work

- Write the lead down **during** the call, not after — two fields, ten seconds.
- Always set a follow-up date. A lead without one belongs to nobody.
- Be honest with **Lost** and its reason. The report is only as truthful as the stages are.
