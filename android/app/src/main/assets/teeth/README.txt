Photographs the tooth chart cuts to shape.

Drop image files in this folder with these exact names (jpg, png or webp).
Each one is optional: a missing file makes the chart draw that treatment
the conventional way instead. Square or nearly square, 300-600 px across.

  gutta_percha.jpg    the obturated root: pink/orange gutta-percha, close up
  crown_zirconia.jpg  a zirconia / all-ceramic crown surface
  crown_pfm.jpg       a porcelain-fused-to-metal crown surface
  crown_metal.jpg     a full metal or gold crown surface
  composite.jpg       a cured composite restoration surface
  amalgam.jpg         an amalgam restoration surface
  implant.jpg         a titanium implant fixture
  veneer.jpg          a porcelain veneer face

The picture is scaled to cover the region (crown, root, or the restoration
patch) and cropped by the tooth outline, so it should be a plain surface
texture with no background, edges or hands in it.
