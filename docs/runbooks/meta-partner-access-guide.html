Connecting a clinic to Alpha on Meta

Each clinic owns its own Meta Business Portfolio. Alpha gets partner access to two things only: the Facebook Page (leads) and the WhatsApp account (bot + webhook). Version 3 · 2026-09-09.

The rule. The clinic owns its Business Portfolio, its Page, its Instagram, its ad account, its card and its WhatsApp number. Alpha never touches ads or money. Alpha is added as a partner on the Page and on the WhatsApp Business Account so that leads and WhatsApp messages flow into the clinic's Alpha account. The clinic can remove the partner at any time.

2. One-time setup on Alpha's side

  • App is Live with the leads and WhatsApp webhooks verified — already done.
  • System-user token exists and never expires, with: pages_show_list, leads_retrieval, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_metadata, whatsapp_business_management, whatsapp_business_messaging — already done. No ads permissions are needed in this model.
  • Business Verification of Alpha's portfolio — recommended, not required for the first clinics. Meta is stricter with unverified partners on WhatsApp assets, and verification is required if you later want the self-serve "Connect" button (Embedded Signup) inside Alpha.
  • Superadmin login ready: the Meta Leads tab and the clinic's Settings → WhatsApp card are where you connect things.
  • A checklist copy of this document per clinic, and the Arabic handout sent 2–3 days before the meeting so they arrive prepared.

3. What the clinic prepares before the meeting

Send them the Arabic handout (last pages). In short, they bring:

  1. The owner's personal Facebook login, on their own laptop or phone. Portfolios are created from a personal account; it should be the owner's, not an employee's who may leave.
  2. Admin access to the clinic's Facebook Page (and the Instagram login if it is not yet connected to the Page).
  3. A SIM for the clinic's WhatsApp line with no WhatsApp account on it, in a phone that receives SMS during the meeting. If the number already has WhatsApp: delete that account in the app first (Settings → Account → Delete my account). The number will be used by the bot from now on; staff chat from the Alpha inbox, not from the WhatsApp app.
  4. A 6-digit PIN they choose for the WhatsApp number (two-step verification). They keep it; Alpha does not.
  5. A payment card for Meta (ads and WhatsApp templates). Any card they have; Alpha never sees or types it. If no card works, ads and reminders wait, everything else proceeds.
  6. Business documents for verification (commercial register or tax card + a utility bill/address). Optional on the day; needed later for more than 250 WhatsApp reminders a day or the green tick.
  7. The names of the two or three treatments they advertise (for the lead-form question, section 6) and who answers leads (that person needs an Alpha user with notifications on).
Alpha staff never type or read the clinic's Facebook password, card number or PIN. During an online meeting the clinic shares their screen and types; on site the clinic owner sits at the keyboard for those three moments. Everything else you may drive.

4. The setup meeting, step by step

CLINIC = clinic types on their own account.   ALPHA = you, on your account. Skip any part they already have.

Part A — Business Portfolio (10 min)

  1. CLINIC Go to business.facebook.com → Create a business portfolio (top-left menu if they already have one for something else). Name = clinic name; their name; a clinic email. Confirm the email from their inbox.
  2. CLINIC Settings → Business info: fill Legal business name, address, phone, website (Facebook Page URL is accepted as website). WhatsApp account creation is refused until these are filled — this is not optional.
  3. CLINIC Settings → Accounts → Pages → Add → Add a Page → choose the clinic's Page. It becomes owned by the portfolio.
  4. CLINIC Settings → Accounts → Instagram accounts → Add → log in to the clinic's Instagram (must be a professional account; switch inside the Instagram app if not). Optional for Alpha, needed for their ads.
  5. CLINIC Settings → Accounts → Ad accounts → Add → Create a new ad account → time zone Cairo, currency EGP (cannot be changed later) → add their card under Billing & payments. Alpha steps away from the screen for the card. This whole step is theirs; if they already run ads, skip.
  6. CLINIC (Recommended, can be later) Settings → Security Centre → Business verification: upload commercial register / tax card. Takes days; it raises WhatsApp limits and allows more numbers.

Part B — Share the Page with Alpha (5 min)

  1. CLINIC Settings → Users → Partners → Add → Give a partner access to your assets.
  2. CLINIC Partner business ID 1135213139685152 → Next.
  3. CLINIC Under Pages, tick the clinic's Page → Full control. If they prefer minimal: Content, Insights, Ads. Do not tick ad accounts or Instagram — Alpha does not need them.
  4. CLINIC Save. The Page now shows under Partners → ألفا with its permissions; that screen is also where they remove it one day.
  5. CLINIC Leads access: Meta Business Suite (business.facebook.com/latest) → choose the Page → Integrations → Leads access. If it shows "Customise access", press it → Confirm. Then the Partners tab → Assign → ألفا لعلاج و تجميل الأسنان. If Leads access was never customised, the default already allows all admins and connected apps — pressing Customise is still the safe move because it lists what has access.

Alternative: ALPHA sends a request instead — Alpha's Business settings → Partners → Add → Ask a partner to assign you their assets → creates a link → the clinic opens it and approves. Same result; useful when the meeting is over the phone.

Part C — WhatsApp Business Account (20 min)

  1. CLINIC Settings → Accounts → WhatsApp accounts → Add → Create a new WhatsApp Business account → name = clinic name → time zone Cairo → currency. If Meta says "policy requirements not met", Business info (Part A step 2) is incomplete.
  2. CLINIC Open WhatsApp Manager (the "Open in WhatsApp Manager" link, or business.facebook.com/wa/manage) → Phone numbers → Add phone number → display name = clinic name as on the Page (Arabic is fine; no emojis, no "official") → category Medical & Health → country Egypt → the SIM's number → verify by SMS → type the code that arrives on the SIM.
  3. CLINIC On the number: Settings → Two-step verification → set PIN. Their PIN; write nothing down for Alpha.
  4. CLINIC WhatsApp Manager → Payment settings → Add payment method → their card. Skip if no card yet; only templates need it.
  5. CLINIC Share it with Alpha: Settings → Users → Partners → ألفا → Assign assets → WhatsApp accounts → tick the new WABA → Full control (Manage WhatsApp business account) → Save. Full control is what lets Alpha's app subscribe to the account and register templates.
  6. CLINIC Read out loud, or screenshot for you, two numbers from WhatsApp Manager: the WhatsApp Business Account ID (on the account overview) and the Phone number ID (on the number's row). These are not secrets.

Part D — Alpha connects (10 min)

  1. ALPHA Alpha's Business settings → Users → System users → Ahmed Tarek → Assign assets → Pages → tick the clinic's Page (it appears with the clinic's business name) → Full control. Then WhatsApp accounts → tick the clinic's WABA → Full control. Without this the token cannot see either asset.
  2. ALPHA Superadmin → Meta Leads tab → the clinic's Page is now in the dropdown → select the clinic → Connect. This subscribes the Page's lead forms to Alpha's webhook. Every future form on the Page delivers automatically.
  3. ALPHA Open the clinic in Alpha → Settings → WhatsApp → "Official WhatsApp (Meta)" card → paste Phone Number ID and WABA ID → Save. The save writes the routing entry and subscribes the Alpha app to the clinic's WABA.
  4. CLINIC In the same card, the Register box: the clinic types their 6-digit PIN → Register. The box shows Meta's exact answer; "133005" means wrong PIN, "already registered" means a WhatsApp app account still exists on the SIM.
  5. ALPHA Templates: npx tsx scripts/register-meta-templates.mts --waba=<clinic WABA id>. They arrive PENDING and are approved by Meta within minutes to 24 h. Reminders wait for approval; the bot does not.
  6. ALPHA Check the clinic's Alpha settings the bot depends on: doctors and working hours filled in (no schedule = the bot will not offer booking), auto-confirm toggle as the clinic wants, opt-out footer on, notification language.

Part E — Test everything before you leave (10 min)

  1. ALPHA Test lead: developers.facebook.com/tools/lead-ads-testing → the clinic's Page → any form (if none exists yet, the clinic creates a quick one in Ads Manager → Instant forms, or you test after their first form) → Create lead → it appears in the clinic's Alpha Leads inbox within seconds, source "Meta ads". Delete the test lead afterwards.
  2. CLINIC Test chat: from a personal phone send "hi" to the clinic number → the bot answers in Arabic with the clinic's name → walk through doctor, day, time → the booking appears in the clinic's Alpha calendar, and the receptionist's phone gets the push.
  3. ALPHA After a template is APPROVED (later the same day): send one reminder from Alpha and confirm delivery. If it fails, the clinic's WABA has no payment method.
  4. Fill the record sheet (section 7). Done.

5. Alpha's side after the meeting — recap checklist

  • Page assigned to system user Ahmed Tarek (Full control).
  • WABA assigned to system user Ahmed Tarek (Full control).
  • Page connected to the clinic in the Meta Leads tab; test lead arrived.
  • Phone Number ID + WABA ID saved in the clinic's Settings → WhatsApp; number registered (status shows connected).
  • Templates registered on the clinic's WABA; at least one APPROVED before the first reminder.
  • Receptionist has an Alpha user, phone push enabled, set as default owner of new leads.
  • Record sheet filed.

6. What to tell the clinic's ad person

Alpha does not run or judge their ads. Two things about the lead form decide whether leads arrive usable; say them once and put them in the handout:

  1. First multiple-choice question = the treatment. e.g. "إيه الخدمة اللي مهتم بيها؟" with short answers (تقويم معدني / تقويم شفاف / زراعة / تبييض / مش عارف ومحتاج استشارة). Alpha reads that answer as the lead's interest and shows it on the card and in the funnel. A "preferred contact time" question is fine, but after it.
  2. Ask for phone number, not email. Prefill fields Full name + Phone number. Egyptian patients skip email and the receptionist calls anyway.

Also useful: the form needs a privacy-policy link (Meta requires it; a PDF is refused). They may use their website's or Alpha's https://alpha-v3-live.vercel.app/privacy. And every new form on a connected Page delivers to Alpha automatically — no need to tell you when they create one.

8. Offboarding — when a clinic leaves

  1. ALPHA Export their patients, leads and chats from Alpha and hand the file over; then use the clinic-deletion flow.
  2. ALPHA Meta Leads tab → disconnect the Page. Settings → WhatsApp → clear the Meta credentials.
  3. CLINIC Settings → Users → Partners → ألفا → Remove partner. That revokes both assets at once. (Alpha can also remove itself from its own Business settings.)
  4. CLINIC WhatsApp number: it is theirs and stays in their WABA. To use it in the normal WhatsApp app again: WhatsApp Manager → the number → Delete, wait a little, then register in the app. To keep using the Cloud API with another provider, they just share the WABA with that provider instead.

ورقة للعيادة: ربط الفيسبوك والواتساب بنظام «ألفا»

حسابك ملكك بالكامل: الصفحة، الإعلانات، الكارت، ورقم الواتساب. ألفا بتاخد صلاحية «شريك» على حاجتين بس: الصفحة والواتساب — عشان العملاء والرسايل يوصلوا للنظام. وتقدر تشيل الصلاحية في أي وقت.

إحنا مش بنعمل إعلاناتك ولا بندفع عنك. الإعلانات من حسابك وبكارتك وبالطريقة اللي تحبها. دورنا إن العميل اللي يسيب رقمه في إعلانك يوصل فوراً لموظف الاستقبال في نظام ألفا، وإن رقم واتساب العيادة يرد أوتوماتيك ويحجز.

جهّز قبل الاجتماع (أونلاين أو في العيادة، حوالي ساعة)

  1. حساب الفيسبوك الشخصي لصاحب العيادة على اللابتوب أو الموبايل بتاعه. (مش حساب موظف ممكن يمشي.)
  2. أدمن على صفحة العيادة، وباسورد الإنستجرام لو مش مربوط بالصفحة.
  3. خط جديد للواتساب مايكونش عليه واتساب. لو عليه واتساب: الإعدادات ← الحساب ← حذف حسابي. الخط ده هيبقى للبوت، والموظفين هيردوا من نظام ألفا مش من تطبيق الواتساب.
  4. رقم سري ٦ أرقام تختاره للخط. احتفظ بيه إنت، ألفا مش هتعرفه.
  5. كارت بنكي للإعلانات ورسايل التذكير (أي كارت شغال مع فيسبوك). ألفا مش هتشوفه ولا تكتبه.
  6. السجل التجاري أو البطاقة الضريبية (اختياري في نفس اليوم؛ لازم بعدين لو عايز أكتر من ٢٥٠ رسالة تذكير في اليوم أو العلامة الخضراء).
  7. أسماء ٢–٣ خدمات بتعمل عليهم إعلانات، واسم الموظف اللي هيرد على العملاء.

اللي هيحصل في الاجتماع

١) حساب الأعمال (Business Portfolio)

  1. افتح business.facebook.com ← إنشاء حساب أعمال ← اسم العيادة وإيميل.
  2. الإعدادات ← معلومات الشركة: الاسم القانوني والعنوان والتليفون والموقع (لينك الصفحة ينفع). من غيرها فيسبوك بيرفض إنشاء حساب الواتساب.
  3. الإعدادات ← الحسابات ← الصفحات ← إضافة ← إضافة صفحة ← صفحة العيادة.
  4. الإعدادات ← الحسابات ← حسابات Instagram ← إضافة.
  5. الإعدادات ← الحسابات ← الحسابات الإعلانية ← إنشاء حساب إعلاني ← الكارت بتاعك (ألفا بتبعد عن الشاشة هنا).

٢) مشاركة الصفحة مع ألفا

  1. الإعدادات ← المستخدمون ← الشركاء (Partners) ← إضافة ← منح شريك حق الوصول إلى الأصول.
  2. رقم الشريك: 1135213139685152
  3. من «الصفحات» علّم على صفحة العيادة ← تحكم كامل ← حفظ. (متعلّمش على الحساب الإعلاني ولا الإنستجرام — ألفا مش محتاجاهم.)
  4. Meta Business Suite ← اختار الصفحة ← Integrations ← Leads access ← تخصيص الوصول ← الشركاء ← أضف «ألفا لعلاج و تجميل الأسنان».

٣) حساب الواتساب

  1. الإعدادات ← الحسابات ← حسابات WhatsApp ← إضافة ← إنشاء حساب WhatsApp Business جديد ← اسم العيادة.
  2. افتح WhatsApp Manager ← أرقام الهاتف ← إضافة رقم ← اسم العرض = اسم العيادة زي الصفحة ← الفئة: طبي وصحي ← الرقم ← كود SMS.
  3. على الرقم: الإعدادات ← التحقق بخطوتين ← الرقم السري ٦ أرقام.
  4. إعدادات الدفع ← إضافة طريقة دفع ← الكارت بتاعك (ينفع بعدين؛ بيلزم بس لرسايل التذكير).
  5. الإعدادات ← المستخدمون ← الشركاء ← ألفا ← تعيين الأصول ← حسابات WhatsApp ← الحساب الجديد ← تحكم كامل ← حفظ.
  6. اقرالنا رقمين من WhatsApp Manager: WhatsApp Business Account ID وPhone number ID. دول مش سر.

٤) ألفا بتربط وبنجرّب

  1. ألفا بتربط الصفحة والواتساب من جهتها (دقايق).
  2. إنت هتكتب الرقم السري بتاعك في خانة «تسجيل» في نظام ألفا (على شاشتك).
  3. بنبعت عميل تجريبي من أداة فيسبوك ← يظهر عندك في «العملاء المحتملين».
  4. بتبعت «hi» من موبايلك لرقم العيادة ← البوت يرد ويحجز ← الحجز يظهر في التقويم.

لموظف الإعلانات عندك: نقطتين في نموذج العملاء (Instant Form)

  1. أول سؤال اختيار من متعدد = الخدمة. مثال: «إيه الخدمة اللي مهتم بيها؟» والإجابات: تقويم معدني / تقويم شفاف / زراعة / تبييض / مش عارف ومحتاج استشارة. ألفا بتقرا الإجابة دي وبتكتبها على كارت العميل. سؤال «إمتى نتواصل معاك؟» ينفع بس بعده.
  2. اطلب رقم الموبايل مش الإيميل. الحقول: الاسم بالكامل + رقم الهاتف.

النموذج محتاج لينك سياسة خصوصية (فيسبوك بيرفض PDF). ممكن تستخدم موقعك أو لينك ألفا: https://alpha-v3-live.vercel.app/privacy — وأي نموذج جديد على الصفحة بيوصل لألفا أوتوماتيك.
