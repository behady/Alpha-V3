# Chapter 3 — The Bot and the AI

English voiceover, burned-in Arabic subtitles. Demo clinic, Arabic UI, 1920x1080.
Beats match `CHAPTERS.botvsai` in `scripts/promo-chapters.mjs` one-to-one, by number.

The two are sold and priced separately, and a dentist who confuses them will be disappointed by
whichever one he bought. The honest line: **the bot is free and never surprises you; the AI costs
a credit and handles what you never wrote down.** They run as one chain — the AI is the last
layer, reached only when nothing cheaper matched.
