# Chapter 3 — The Bot and the AI

English voiceover, burned-in Arabic subtitles. Demo clinic, Arabic UI, 1920x1080.
Beats match `CHAPTERS.botvsai` in `scripts/promo-chapters.mjs` one-to-one, by number.

The pitch: **the bot is free and never surprises you; the AI is a hire.** Three jobs, each one a
setting — receptionist (it answers and books), salesman (`botCoaching`, briefed like a new
employee), and first line on symptoms (`botClinicalMode: "dentist"` — it talks the patient through
the symptom the way a dentist would, then offers the appointment, instead of "someone will call
you back").

What stays true regardless: it only states facts the clinic supplied, it never quotes a final
price for treatment that needs an exam, and complaints go to a person.
