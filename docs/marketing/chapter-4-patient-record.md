# Chapter 4 — The Patient Record

English voiceover, burned-in Arabic subtitles. Demo clinic, Arabic UI, 1920x1080.
Beats match `CHAPTERS.record` in `scripts/promo-chapters.mjs` one-to-one, by number.

The argument: a dentist's real filing problem is not storage, it's **recall in thirty seconds with
a patient in the chair**. Every beat is one question a dentist actually asks mid-appointment —
what did we do last time, what did we agree, what do they owe, which tooth.
