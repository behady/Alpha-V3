# Chapter 2 — WhatsApp

English voiceover, burned-in Arabic subtitles. Demo clinic, Arabic UI, 1920x1080.
Beats match `CHAPTERS.whatsapp` in `scripts/promo-chapters.mjs` one-to-one, by number.

Built from conversations that already happened. Nothing here sends a message: replying inside a
real thread goes out through the live WhatsApp channel, and the built-in playground — which would
have let the bot answer live and harmlessly — cannot reply until the `whatsappBot` add-on is
enabled on the demo clinic.
